Frequently Asked Questions

What is vehicle mileage?

Vehicle mileage tells you how far your vehicle can travel using one litre of fuel. It is usually measured in km/L.

How is mileage calculated?

Mileage is calculated by dividing the distance travelled by the amount of fuel used.

What is cost per kilometre?

Cost per kilometre tells you approximately how much fuel costs for every kilometre you travel.

Can I use this calculator for cars and bikes?

Yes. You can use the calculator for cars, motorcycles, scooters and other fuel-powered vehicles.
